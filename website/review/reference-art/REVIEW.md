# Reference artwork correction — 15 November 2026

The previous Chat page still had an oversized synthetic laptop silhouette,
separated network heading/steps, flat archive trays and three closed cabinets.
The user's follow-up identifies sections 2, 3, 5 and 6 and supplies the selected
salon concept plus the binding garden-network replacement. This correction
rebuilds those four scenes and their surrounding composition.

- **Signal foundation:** a naturally proportioned physical laptop on an ivory
  writing desk, with its full keyboard, hinge and bezel. The synthetic English
  conversation is real HTML/CSS, scaled within the photographed 16:10 screen.
  Concise roles and the original Signal mark sit on the quiet left side.
- **Messaging:** one continuous twilight garden with the heading, three steps
  and caption integrated into the scene. Small gold points sit precisely on
  the eight pavilion roof tips. Fine connections originate at the two envelopes.
  The 15 edges and both internally disjoint routes remain unchanged; mobile
  retains its own complete layout, with restrained light points and labels.
- **Recovery:** small ornate walnut/brass chests between two actual laptops,
  including a muted unavailable storage peer. Four native-text steps and the
  scoped trial evidence strip remain visible below the devices. A subtle dark
  gradient protects text contrast without obscuring the chests.
- **Reciprocal storage:** two open, ornamented cabinets, warm inner compartments
  and reciprocal arrows. The calculation sits beside the doors at normal
  desktop sizes and below the scene on mobile or when enlarged text needs space.

The generated scenes are standalone illustrations, not cropped screenshots of
the concept. New master provenance/prompts are recorded in
[reference-refinement.json](../../masters/reference-refinement.json); dimensions,
crops, exported bytes and hashes are recorded in the asset manifest. Masters and
review evidence are excluded from the published artifact. Older masters remain
available in Git history. The shared theme and original branding are unchanged.

All **50 pre-existing paragraphs are retained verbatim**. The short role labels
are simplified; full explanations remain accessible through native disclosures.
The added recovery explanation distinguishes the illustrative storage model
from the documented trial with three provider environments on **one machine**.
The four decorative chests are not a new provider-count or availability claim.
Development badges, private-cache/training exclusion, owner-held credentials,
illustration captions and independent affiliation remain explicit. No native
Signal/Core trial or integration-readiness claim is added by this visual change.
