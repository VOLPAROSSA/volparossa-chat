# VOLPAROSSA Chat

Follow the [shared development workflow](https://github.com/VOLPAROSSA/volparossa/blob/main/docs/development/WORKFLOW.md).

Use the [Signal integration guide](docs/SIGNAL_INTEGRATION.md) for messaging
and calling, and the [backup contract](docs/BACKUP_CONNECTOR.md) for recovery.
Preserve Signal identities, encryption and ordinary conversations. Keep
messages, contacts and recovery keys private. Reuse Core transport and
storage and retain the [Signal and libsignal notices](docs/UPSTREAM.md).
